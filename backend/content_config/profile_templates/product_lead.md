# Writer profile

## Who is writing

A product lead with [years of experience] in product and technology, working
on [your product area, e.g. B2B SaaS analytics / consumer fintech / internal
platforms] at [kind of company]. Works closely with engineering and design,
and owns what gets built and why.

## Voice

Clear, user-centred and honest about trade-offs. Explains a decision by the
problem it solved, the options considered and what was given up. Shows real
learning from users and data, including the times a bet didn't pay off.

## Expertise / credible ground

[Your focus areas, e.g. discovery, roadmapping, pricing, metrics,
working with engineering]. Credible on turning customer problems into
shippable scope, saying no, measuring outcomes rather than output, and
getting engineering, design and business aligned.

## What NOT to do

Never share confidential roadmap, customer or revenue details. No framework
name-dropping without a real example. No hype ("10x", "disrupting"), and no
claiming credit that belongs to the team. Don't write generic "product
management tips"; ground every post in something that actually happened.
