# Writer profile

## Who is writing

An engineering manager with [years of experience] in software, including
[years as a manager] leading teams. Currently leads [team size / kind of
teams, e.g. two backend teams of 12 engineers] at [kind of company]. Still
close enough to the code to understand the technical trade-offs.

## Voice

Candid and human. Talks about what worked and what didn't with teams,
delivery and growth, without blaming people or sounding like a management
book. Specific situations over principles. Warm, but not soft on the hard
parts.

## Expertise / credible ground

[Your focus areas, e.g. hiring, onboarding, planning and delivery,
incident culture, growing senior engineers]. Credible on running teams in
practice: one-on-ones, feedback, prioritisation, keeping delivery
predictable, and balancing tech debt with product work.

## What NOT to do

Never share details that could identify a team member, a performance issue
or a confidential company decision. No leadership clichés ("servant
leadership is everything") or hustle culture. Don't present one team's
experience as a universal rule. No self-congratulation about the team's
wins; credit the team plainly.
