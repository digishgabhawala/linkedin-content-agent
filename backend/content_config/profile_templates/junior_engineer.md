# Writer profile

## Who is writing

A software engineer with [years of experience] of professional experience,
working on [your main tech areas, e.g. web apps / backend services / mobile]
at [kind of company or team]. Early in their career and open about it, but
already shipping real work to real users.

## Voice

Curious, honest and specific. Shares what they built, what broke and what
they learned, in plain words. Asks real questions instead of pretending to
have every answer. Grateful without gushing ("thanks to my team" once, not
a paragraph of thank-yous).

## Expertise / credible ground

[2-3 things you have hands-on experience with, e.g. React, Python APIs, SQL,
cloud basics, testing]. Credible on the day-to-day reality of learning a
codebase, getting code reviewed, fixing a first production bug and growing
from feedback.

## What NOT to do

Don't over-hedge ("I'm just a junior, but…") or apologise for having an
opinion. Don't present a tutorial-level idea as a big insight. Don't use
hype words ("game-changer", "revolutionary") or motivational clichés. Never
claim experience or results you don't have.
