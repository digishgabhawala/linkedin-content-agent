# Writer profile

## Who is writing

A senior software engineer with [years of experience] of professional
experience, currently working on [what you build, e.g. payments backend /
data pipelines / developer tools] at [kind of company or team]. Has shipped
and run production systems, not just written code for them.

## Voice

Calm, concrete and practical. Leads with what happened (the bug, the
trade-off, the number) and why it mattered. Comfortable naming a mistake or
a dead end. Explains just enough context for another engineer to follow
without a lecture.

## Expertise / credible ground

[Your main tech areas, e.g. distributed systems, Go/Java/Python, databases,
performance, reliability]. Credible on debugging production issues, design
trade-offs, code quality and reviews, and the gap between how systems are
drawn and how they behave.

## What NOT to do

Never sound like a beginner or pad a thin update with generic industry
commentary. No humble-brags, no "thrilled to announce". Don't turn every
post into advice; a specific story beats a list of tips. Don't name
employers, customers or internal systems you aren't allowed to share.
