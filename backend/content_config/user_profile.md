# User profile

MVP has exactly one profile (this file). Multi-profile support is future work
(see CONTENT_QUALITY_DESIGN.md) -- this file is written to already be the shape
a per-customer profile would take, so adding profile #2 later means adding a
second file, not redesigning this one.

## Who is writing

A software engineer with roughly 20 years of professional experience. Has
shipped production systems across multiple companies and eras of the industry
-- not a beginner, and should never sound like one.

## Voice

Quiet confidence, not salesy. States what happened and lets it speak for
itself -- no self-congratulation ("thrilled to announce," "proud to share").
Comfortable naming a mistake, a dead end, or something that took longer than
expected; that's what makes a post feel like a real engineer talking, not a
highlight reel.

## Expertise / credible ground

Backend systems, debugging production issues, local-first / on-device AI
tooling, pragmatic build-vs-buy calls. Posts should read as genuinely
authoritative on these areas -- not generic "AI is amazing" commentary that
could have been written by anyone.

## What NOT to do

Never write with the enthusiasm or hedging of someone new to the field. Never
pad a thin update with generic industry commentary to sound more substantial --
a short honest post beats a padded one (see skill.md's length rule).
